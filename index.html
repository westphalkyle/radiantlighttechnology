Good questions.
Straightforward answers.

Every business starts somewhere different. Here’s what working together can look like.

Where do we start?

With a conversation about your business, your current technology, and what you’d like to improve. From there, we can identify a useful next step and discuss the scope of work.

Can you work with our existing IT team?

Yes. We can complement your internal team with an outside perspective, focused planning, or help on a specific initiative. We’ll define roles and responsibilities together.

Do we need to know exactly what we need?

No. If you know something could work better but aren’t sure where to begin, an assessment can help clarify the challenges and identify practical priorities.

How are projects scoped and priced?

Scope and pricing depend on your needs, systems, and goals. We’ll discuss deliverables, responsibilities, and costs before you commit to a project.
